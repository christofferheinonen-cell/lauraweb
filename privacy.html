2. What personal data we collect

When you enquire through our enquiry form, by email or by phone: your name, email address, phone number (if given), location, the service you are interested in, and anything you choose to tell us in your message.

When we work together: your contact details, the address of the property, practical details needed to carry out the project (such as access arrangements and your preferences), the agreed scope and price, and invoicing and payment information.

Photographs: we may photograph spaces before and after a project to plan and document the work. We publish photographs (for example on this website or social media) only with your explicit consent, and never in a way that reveals your identity or the location of your home.

When you visit this website: our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit in server logs, for security and to keep the website running. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and its fonts are hosted on our own server.

Please do not send us sensitive personal data (such as health information) through the enquiry form.

3. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • Responding to your enquiry and preparing a quote — to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Carrying out the agreed project — performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Invoicing and bookkeeping — compliance with our legal obligations, such as the Finnish Accounting Act (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Keeping in touch with existing clients, handling feedback and protecting our legal rights — our legitimate interest in running and developing our business (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Publishing photographs of your spaces — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Website server logs — our legitimate interest in keeping the website secure and functioning (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Providing your data is voluntary, but we cannot respond to an enquiry or carry out a project without the necessary contact and project details. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 12 months of our last contact.
  • Client data is kept for the duration of our client relationship and for up to three years afterwards, in line with the general limitation period for claims.
  • Accounting records are kept for the periods required by the Finnish Accounting Act (currently up to ten years).
  • Published photographs are removed if you withdraw your consent.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf and under our instructions, such as our email and IT service providers, the website hosting provider, the service that delivers enquiry form messages to us, and our accounting firm. We may also disclose data to authorities where required by law.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases we make sure the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, where there is no legal reason for us to keep it;
  • restrict or object to our processing of your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To use your rights, email us at laura.peltola@hotmail.com. We will respond within one month.

If you believe we have processed your data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish supervisory authority, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

7. Security and confidentiality

We treat everything we learn about you and your home as confidential. Access to personal data is limited to those who need it to do their work, and we use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
